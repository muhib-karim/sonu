Dictate into any app. Nothing leaves your machine.

SONU is a free, open-source voice typing app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Press a shortcut, speak, and your words are typed into whatever you are using, transcribed locally by Parakeet, Whisper or Moonshine.

Why SONU

100% offline by default

Transcription runs on your device. No account, no subscription, no audio uploaded unless you turn on a cloud engine.

Real-time

Live preview while you speak, then a full-accuracy final pass.

Types anywhere

Browser, IDE, email, Slack, Discord, Word: anywhere you can type.

50+ languages

Parakeet V3 auto-detects; Whisper covers 99 languages.

Context-aware

Adapts tone and formatting to the app you are typing in.

Optional AI clean-up

Removes filler words and fixes grammar with an OpenAI-compatible provider of your choice.
